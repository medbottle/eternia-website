How To Play

Welcome to Meranthe

Getting started is quick and free. Download the launcher, create your character, and follow the in-game tutorial to learn the basics.

1

Download

Install the game launcher to connect to Meranthe. We recommend using the official launcher rather than connecting manually.

Download Launcher
2

Create your character

Choose your starting location and race, then write a description for your character. You can update your description later from the in-game options menu.

Read the Player Guide
3

Play!

After creating your character, complete the tutorial quests with the Tutorial NPC to learn controls, shopping, combat, dungeons, and other core systems. Join the community for help, events, and roleplay.

Join the Discord

Meet other players, ask questions, and keep up with events and announcements.

Browse the forums

Read guides, find guilds and factions, and explore community discussions.
